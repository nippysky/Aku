Radically simple money tracking

No bank connections, no jargon, no clutter. Akù is built around one honest idea: money comes in, money goes out — and you deserve to see it clearly.

⚡

Log in seconds

A floating action button puts expense and income logging one tap away, from anywhere in the app.

📅

Bills that remind you

Due-date reminders at 14, 7, 3 and 1 day out. Bills count as expenses the moment they're due — no double bookkeeping.

🎯

Goals with a destination

Attach the actual account you're saving into — bank, PiggyVest, anywhere — and track pace, projections and history per goal.

📊

Analytics that teach

Cash-flow charts, category donuts and your savings rate over 1–12 months. See the mathematics behind your money.

🔁

Recurring on autopilot

Salaries, rent, subscriptions — logged automatically on schedule, with optional auto-contribution to goals.

🔔

Nudges that fit your day

Smart, personal reminders from morning to bedtime — budget alerts, streaks, milestones, and a goodnight check-in.

Private by design

Akù never connects to your bank and never sees your balances. What you log is encrypted on your device before it ever leaves it.

End-to-end encryption

AES-256-GCM with a key only your devices hold. Our servers store ciphertext they cannot read.

Device-level lock

Face ID, fingerprint, or your phone's PIN protect the app. Nothing extra to remember.

Real-time sync

Log on your iPhone, watch it appear on your Android. Encrypted deltas, instantly delivered.

Your data, your call

Export everything as a PDF statement, or delete your account and every record in one tap.
